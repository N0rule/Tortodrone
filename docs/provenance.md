## Third-party rights

Third-party software is not relicensed by the Tortodrone notice. See the
[dependency notices](../src/main/resources/THIRD-PARTY.md) and the bundled
license texts under [licenses](../licenses).

Original Spiral Knights game content, including artwork, audio, text and captured
scene data, remains the property of its rights holders. The Tortodrone license
cannot grant rights to that material. Bundled scene data is provided for
interoperability testing and development; it is not offered under the PolyForm
license. Review the rights position for any scene, definition or asset before
redistributing it, and do not add game assets, client binaries, extracted
artwork or private captures to the repository.

WebUI artwork produced from an installed client stays in local deployment
directories and is not committed or included in release archives.

## Contribution expectations

Implement interoperability and observed behavior using Tortodrone's own design.
Do not copy recovered proprietary implementation, leaked server code, assets or
text into contributions without established permission. Contributions should
describe the behavior they implement and the evidence supporting it, without
including restricted third-party material.
